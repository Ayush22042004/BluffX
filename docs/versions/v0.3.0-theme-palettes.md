# v0.3.0 coordinated theme palettes

Date: 2026-11-13. Local update; not deployed. Version remains 0.3.0.

Each theme now combines multiple colors with distinct roles rather than applying a single hue everywhere:

| Theme | Foundation | Primary | Secondary | Highlight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blue (default) | Midnight navy `#080f1c` | Electric blue `#50aaff` | Cyan `#55dfdb` | Warm gold `#f5cb8a` |
| Pink | Deep plum `#140e1c` | Rose `#ff79bc` | Violet `#c1a0ff` | Peach `#ffc6a1` |
| Emerald | Forest charcoal `#0b1515` | Emerald `#62dfae` | Teal `#77d2e0` | Champagne `#eed6a0` |

New semantic tokens in `client/src/theme-palettes.css` distinguish surfaces, raised areas, borders, text, buttons, secondary details, highlights and scenic tint. Foundation and text colors in `theme-colors.css` are less saturated. Updated `index.css` applies these roles to navigation, panels, forms, room previews, gameplay controls, focus rings and the theme picker. Preferences preview four palette colors and describe their combinations.

Blue remains the default. Existing saved choices and browser persistence are compatible. Card suits, warning/error meanings, player images, layout and game rules are preserved. No migration, backend changes, environment setting or dependency added.
